Outputs: plug in and test the servos (then the motors)

Plug the 6 servos and 3 ESCs into the right Pixhawk outputs, set the switches, check the settings, then test every mode on the desk. Follow parts A to H in order. Props off the whole time.

Status now:
  • ✅ All servos and the 3 ESCs are plugged in and move.
  • ✅ Switches set: SWA arm, SWB return home, SWC 3 modes.
  • ✅ The Pixhawk arms on the desk. ESCs calibrated.
  • ✅ Tilt servos fixed: motors point up in QStabilize and QHover, forward in FBWA.
  • ⏳ Tail motor keeps running in FBWA on the desk: under investigation (Part G).
  • ⏳ Motor test, one motor at a time. Spin direction of each motor.
  • ⏳ Compass calibration again, outdoors, for a green bar.
  • ⏳ SWB (return home) test outdoors, with GPS.
Latest backup: params_latest2.params. The tilt fix (SERVO9_REVERSED 0, SERVO10_REVERSED 1) is not in it yet: save a new backup (Q logo → Vehicle Configuration → Parameters → Tools → Save to file).
Safety: propellers OFF on all 3 motors for this whole guide. Plug the LiPo only when a step says so. Keep fingers away from the tilt mounts: when the servos get power they can jump to an end.

Part A: Wiring

A
  1. Unplug the USB and the LiPo, so the Pixhawk is off.
  2. Plug direction: signal wire (orange/yellow/white) on top, red (+) in the middle, brown/black (−) at the bottom, next to the board's base. The top row is the side with the labels "MAIN OUT" / "AUX OUT".
  3. Plug every servo and ESC as in the table above.
  4. ESC red wire: keep it only on the tail ESC (MAIN 8). On MAIN 5 and MAIN 6: lift the plug's small plastic tab with a needle, pull the red wire's pin out, tape it. Two ESC power outputs together fight each other.
  5. Power the servo rail. The Pixhawk does not power the servo pins from USB or the power module. Pick one:
    OptionHow
    1. Tail ESCThe tail ESC's red wire (MAIN 8) gives 5 V when the LiPo is plugged in.
    2. PDB 5 VA lead from the PDB's 5 V pads to a free + / − pair on the rail (for example MAIN 7, signal pin empty). ⚠️ Never the 12 V output: it destroys the servos.
    For flying, a separate 5–6 V UBEC of 3 A or more is the safest (6 servos can draw 2–3 A together).
Before the tilt servos get power for the first time, set their limits (SERVO9_*, SERVO10_* in Part C), so the mounts don't hit their stops.

Part D: Calibrations

1

microSD card: on the computer, format it FAT32 (cards up to 32 GB; bigger cards: exFAT). Put it back with the Pixhawk off.

The message Logging failed is gone.
2

Compass: Q logo → Vehicle Configuration → Sensors → Compass → OK. Turn the plane slowly in every direction (nose up, nose down, each side, upside down) until Completed. Then Q logo → Vehicle Configuration → Parameters → Tools → Reboot Vehicle (restart the Pixhawk).

Quality bar yellow = fine for the desk. Before the first flight, do it again outdoors, away from metal (cars, tools), until it is green.
3

Keep it still at startup: after every power-up, don't touch the plane for 15 s, until QGC says ArduPilot Ready. It measures its gyros then.

4

ESC calibration (each ESC learns: 1000 µs = stop, 2000 µs = full). Needed once. Uncalibrated ESCs spin by themselves or seem dead.

  1. Battery unplugged, USB only, props off.
  2. Q logo → Vehicle Configuration → Parameters: Q_ESC_CAL = 1 (calibration mode) → Save.
  3. ARMING_SKIPCHK = 400 (also skips the voltage checks, so it arms without the battery) → Save.
  4. SWC up (QStabilize). Safety button until its light is solid. SWA down → QGC shows Armed.
  5. Left stick full up.
  6. Plug the battery. ESCs beep twice = full throttle saved.
  7. Within about 2 s: left stick full down. 3 short beeps + 1 long = stop saved.
  8. SWA up (disarm).
  9. Put back: Q_ESC_CAL = 0, ARMING_SKIPCHK = 16 → Save each.

Part E: Arming checklist

E
  1. Transmitter ON first (switches up, throttle down). Receiver light solid red = linked; blinking = no link.
  2. Plug the plane (USB, then LiPo).
  3. Keep it still 15 s.
  4. Safety button (small red button on its own cable, port SWITCH): hold 3 s until the light is solid. Blinking = locked. It locks again after every reboot.
  5. QGC top bar shows Ready To Fly. Close the Radio page if it is open.
  6. SWB up. SWA down → QGC shows Armed.
  7. At the end: SWA up, unplug the plane first, then switch the transmitter off.

Won't arm? The Pixhawk shows why. Read the message: Fly screen → click the status text in the top bar (or the megaphone icon).

Part F: Test each mode (props off)

Armed, safety button solid. Watch the front motors and the top bar of QGC.

In drone modes the front motors make small opposite tilts (up to ±5°, bigger at low throttle). That is how the plane turns its nose. Hand test: turn the nose by hand → the two front motors tilt opposite each other.

Wrong tilt direction?

If the motors point forward in QStabilize and rise in FBWA, the tilt servos work backwards. Q logo → Vehicle Configuration → Servo Outputs → flip the Reversed box on rows 9 and 10. Final: row 9 off, row 10 on.

A surface moves the wrong way? Q logo → Vehicle Configuration → Servo Outputs → tick Reversed on its row, test again. Drawings: stick controls.

How to read Servo Outputs

Each row shows the live Position (µs) of one output: rows 1–4 = wing and tail servos, rows 5, 6, 8 = motors, rows 9, 10 = tilts. The numbers on rows 9 and 10 changed when Reversed was flipped (before: about 1847 / 857 for "straight up"). So don't trust old numbers: check the motors with your eyes: up in Q modes, forward in FBWA.

Part H: Pre-flight checklist

Before the first hover:
  • ARMING_SKIPCHK back to 0
  • Q_M_THST_HOVER ≈ 0.37 (the Pixhawk relearns it)
  • Q_ASSIST_SPEED = 5
  • New backup saved with the tilt fix
  • Tilts: SWC up = motors straight up, SWC down = forward
  • Compass calibration redone outdoors, quality bar green
  • Transmitter on first, then the plane
  • Plane still ~15 s after power-up, until ArduPilot Ready
  • Safety button solid
  • Props on the correct motors
  • SWB tested outdoors (QRTL shown)
