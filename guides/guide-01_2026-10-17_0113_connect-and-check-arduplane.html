"QuadPlane": why that word, when we only have 3 motors?

QuadPlane is just ArduPlane's name for its VTOL feature. It does not mean 4 motors. It's the part of ArduPlane that lets a plane take off and land vertically with lift motors, then fly on its wings. It supports many motor layouts: quad, hexa, octa, tailsitter, tricopter and tilt-rotor.

Your VTOL Y3 is a tilt-rotor tricopter QuadPlane:

PartServo?Job
Front left + front right motors✅ 1 tilt servo eachLift (pointing up) ↔ thrust (pointing forward)
Tail motor❌ noneLift in drone mode only, off in airplane mode
Main wing, left + right✅ 1 servo eachAilerons: roll in airplane mode
Tail wings, left + right (2 small wings at the back)✅ 1 servo eachV-tail (confirmed): pitch = both move together, yaw = they move opposite.

Total: 6 servos = 2 tilt + 2 ailerons + 2 tail wings.

PhaseFront motorsTail motorWho controls the aircraft
Drone mode (takeoff / hover / landing): Q modes like QSTABILIZE, QHOVER, QLANDTilted up, providing liftOn, providing liftThe 3 motors (with the tilt servos for yaw)
TransitionTilt from up → forwardSlowing downMixed: motors + wings, until airspeed is reached
Airplane mode: MANUAL, FBWA, CRUISE, AUTO…Forward, providing thrustOffWings: ailerons + 2 tail wings

In ArduPlane this is configured with a few Q_ parameters. These are for reference only; don't set anything now:

ParameterMeaning for your Y3
Q_ENABLE = 1Turn on the VTOL (QuadPlane) feature of ArduPlane
Q_FRAME_CLASS = 7Lift-motor layout = Tricopter (3 motors)
Q_TILT_ENABLE = 1Some motors tilt
Q_TILT_MASK = 3Which motors tilt: motor 1 + motor 2 = the two front motors (1 + 2 = 3)
Q_TILT_TYPEHow they tilt (continuous, binary or vectored). Vectored means the left and right tilt servos move separately to steer yaw. We pick the type in a later guide.

Without QuadPlane enabled, ArduPlane treats the aircraft as a normal airplane and would never lift it vertically.

Output map: what each pin on the Pixhawk does

On a Pixhawk 2.4.8, MAIN OUT 1–8 = SERVO1–8 and AUX OUT 1–6 = SERVO9–14. So the old setup expects:

Pixhawk pinParameterFunctionPlug in here
MAIN 1SERVO1AileronAileron servo (one wing)
MAIN 2SERVO2AileronAileron servo (other wing)
MAIN 3SERVO3VTailLeftLeft tail wing servo
MAIN 4SERVO4VTailRightRight tail wing servo
MAIN 5SERVO5Motor1ESC of the front RIGHT motor
MAIN 6SERVO6Motor2ESC of the front LEFT motor
MAIN 7SERVO7Disabled(empty)
MAIN 8SERVO8Motor4ESC of the TAIL motor
AUX 1SERVO9TiltMotorFrontLeftTilt servo, front LEFT motor
AUX 2SERVO10TiltMotorFrontRightTilt servo, front RIGHT motor
AUX 3–6SERVO11–14Disabled(empty)

In ArduPilot's tricopter layout, Motor1 = front right, Motor2 = front left, Motor4 = tail (there's no Motor3). That's why Q_TILT_MASK = 3 (motors 1 + 2) means "the two front motors". All 6 servos + 3 ESCs = 9 outputs are assigned, which is exactly your hardware. This answers the tail question: the old setup treats your 2 tail wings as a V-tail (please still confirm by looking at the plane).
